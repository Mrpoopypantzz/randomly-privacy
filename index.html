Randomly: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Randomly is an app that picks a random friend for you to call. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything stays on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts. We never receive, collect, sell or share your information.

What the app uses

  • Your contacts (read only). The app asks to see your contacts so you can choose friends to pick from. Names and numbers are read on your phone only. They are never sent anywhere. The app never changes your contacts.
  • Notifications. If you turn on the daily reminder, the app sends you a reminder on your phone. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Start-up signal. Android forgets reminders when a phone restarts. The app listens for "the phone just restarted" so it can set your reminder up again.

What the app saves on your phone

Your groups, your "don't pick" list, your settings, and which friends you chose, saved in a small file inside the app. Deleting the app deletes this file.

What the app never does

  • It never reads your call history or messages.
  • It never makes a call by itself. When you tap Call or WhatsApp, your phone's own call screen or WhatsApp opens, and you decide.
  • It has no ads and no tracking.
  • It does not need the internet to work.

Children

Randomly is not made for children under 13.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
